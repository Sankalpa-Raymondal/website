# Who am I?
*October 21, 2026*
## Any Questions?
Every now and then when I go through something challenging or when I do something negative aura, a question props up in my mind. What am I doing? Who am I? I think they are interesting questions. They are quite pertinent for me.

Physically speaking, I am an Indian man. A short one, with a slight M pattern balding. These days I have long hair, up to my shoulders which I usually tie into a ponytail. Haha. Younger me would probably never have predicted this. I am also quite chubby, especially in my belly area. I did have a phase where I lost weight by regularly going to the gym and by eating only salad, but I love food too much to go back to that. I am also bespectacled, and my spectacles are quite old. I have very strong power on my eyes, with -5 for both eyes and with astigmatism. Without my glasses I am blind. I am quite hairy too, fitting to the Indian man image. 

I never care about looks that much. Function first, and then we can look into form. But that does not help in getting a particular type of attention, so I am trying to improve on it. I got a haircut and I am getting new spectacles. Incremental changes make disruptive revolutions, or something like that eh? I read from the book on cancer "The Emperor of All Maladies". I am a bit disappointed in my clothes. I try to follow my grandfathers' style, but that isn't really modern is it? But what to do, I am just that type of a person. 

What am I doing? I am currently a Master's student in SUTD, working in a robotics lab. I am over chasing money and do not want the stuff that everyone wants. I am in pursuit of new experiences, and hopefully better experiences. I am following the path of truth and of knowledge, or atleast trying to do so. I do not have much plans ahead, but I have dreams. I am confident that I can manifest them into reality with enough time.

Who am I? Nationality wise, I am Indian. However, I grew up outside of India for most of my life, which gives me the identity of a Non Resident Indian. I believe I developed most of my personality when I was in high-school in Qatar. I am a man, and a single one. I never really had a girlfriend, but I have liked girls. As is the story for most people like me, the girls I liked did not really like me back. But to be honest at those times, I probably did not deserve these people. And the people I liked have a lot of flaws too. Overall, I believe I can live life very well even without them. Haha. I hope one of these days I can find someone nice too. But there is lot of work to be done on myself before I feel I am a polished individual you know. 

I am an Engineer who completed his bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering. I worked for some time in a multinational company. I am familiar with the worlds of academics and of technology. I like to say I am a curious person, which I developed myself into to avoid what I consider one of the worst things in the world: boredom. Then it would also be pertinent to say that I am a learner, because I believe in myself that I can do it and then I can do it. And a big way of learning is by reading, which I am a huge fan of. Eveytime I read a book, a good book especially, I acquire a new way of thinking. Its like a cyborg adding a new technical module into their body. 

Where am I? I am currently in Singapore. And if you are reading this, I now life rent free in your mind. I also believe that if you like me, I live in your heart. I hate myself for writing that. Oh yeah, I live on Earth. I am in peace too, and I am no longer in pain like how I was when I was studying in FIITJEE during highschool. 

How am I doing? I am doing good today. The Gods have been kind to me, probably because I am very lucky. Things work out for me... until it doesn't. And then something else does.  I am also quite lazy. I have been getting away with it for 24 years now. I think I can get away with it for a while more. I am not lazy when I have set my mind to do it. But I believe that most things in life are really not worth it so I don't bother to do too many difficult things. I mean, I am getting better in guitar and in basketball I think. I even managed to hit a three-pointer one of these days! So yeah. Life is going good. I got a masters with scholarship. I make new friends, and I keep in touch with my old ones. I live with my sister, who is hilarious and quite brilliant. 

How dare I? Look, I like to consider myself brave. I think I can do whatever is needed if it is needed. I still do feel nervous I try to ask people things sometimes. But I have overcome it before. And as for breaking some of the rules we set ourselves, I feel that Free Will is something we all have. I have not broken any rule yet that I did not face forward while I was facing its consequences. I usually have a clear conscience, because I feel that I am giving my best. 

Beans? yes. Infact, I am going to eat beans with tacos in Guzman Y Gomez today. Wish me luck.
