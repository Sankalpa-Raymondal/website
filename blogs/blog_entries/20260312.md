But in our current society, the makers of food are in the most rough possible lifestyle from what it looks like. Okay that is not true, getting on a tractor and blocking Delhi looks pretty cool. But atleast in my family, being a farmer is not looked highly upon. I would need to be physically fit and be able to do a lot of things. And I believe I wont be the only one with this problem. There are 6 million people in Singapore who are not farmers and need food from outside.
