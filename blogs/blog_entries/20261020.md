As I sat there, I tried to absorb what is going on in the room.

![Generic Durga Maa Idol](images_blog/durgamaa.jpg.webp)

There was ofcourse the Durga Maa idol. Let me describe it for you a bit more. Every year for 10 days in October, the Goddess Durga (who is married to Shiva, a weed-smoking, super powerful, short tempered God who lives in the Himalaya mountains) comes back to her father's home for a sort of vacation, which is Bengal. She comes with her four children, Ganesha (the elephant God), Kartikeya (the Warrior God, not to be confused with the elephant God), Saraswati (the Goddess of knowledge, and she holds a stringed instrument called the Veena) and Lakshmi (the Goddess of wealth, also called Lokkhi by Bengalis because we are cute). Why does Maa Durga only get to come back to her father's house only for 10 days? Why doesn't the all powerful Goddess who defeated one of the most difficult Demons get to go to her "father's home" whenever she wants? I am yet to do the research. But as per my knowledge, I have presented you my version of the story.

I mentioned a demon right? He is an Asura called Mahishasur, which kind of means Buffalo Demon. As the lore goes, he went through a lot of penance and stuff to ask Brahma, one of the three OG Gods, to be invincible to Devas (Gods), men and all animals. (Or something like that.) God Brahma was like "its all cool bro" and this Mahishasur guy got really annoying. Afterall, he was quite invincible infront of all the people infront of him. Luckily, there was sort of a loop hole where he is technically vulnerable to a woman. I would assume that there was a strong patriarchy thing on going at that time, because there was no Goddesses around. So all the Devas joined their powers, created Durga (? unsure about this) and litereally "armed" her up with like 10 different weapons because she had 10 arms. And then she GirlBossed the heck out of Mahishasur, as evident from the idol where she tears out the entrails of a green looking dude in the bottom. This was apparently very cool and everyone (atleast the Hindus) liked it so much to celebrate Durga Puja, or Navratri. 

I have so many questions. How is it that there is only one Goddess I know who can fight in Hindu mythology? How is it there is literally no other woman in the staff of the Devas?
